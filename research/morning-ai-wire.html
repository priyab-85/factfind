Aug 2agentic.aiRegulatory

EU AI Act high-risk provisions became enforceable

Risk management, human oversight, and conformity assessment obligations are now live, alongside transparency rules requiring chatbots to identify themselves. Financial advice tooling sits close to the high-risk line, and "human oversight" is exactly the review-gate argument — the obligation now has teeth.

Aug 2026kitces.com

Robinhood Cortex for Advisors arrives free inside TradePMR Fusion

Portfolio insight, tax-optimization surfacing, and meeting prep and recap — bundled at no extra cost to Fusion advisors. Meeting prep and recap being given away is the notable part: it prices a category that standalone tools charge for.

Aug 11fintech.global

AI won't replace wealth advisors — it will redefine them

Adoption is "middle-to-middle" rather than end-to-end: advisors are comfortable with note-taking and form completion, and stop well short of letting AI touch the record. A useful sanity check on where the market actually is.

LSEG: 73% of wealth and asset execs call AI critical to their future

Framed around closing the advice gap rather than cutting cost. Good source for the "why now" slide if you ever need to justify this class of tooling internally.
