Generated by `parse_instacart.py`; raw per-item history in `aggregate.json`.

**Pickup store:** Whole Foods Market — One Wall Street, 66 Broadway, New York NY 10005.
In-store pickup only (no curbside, no designated parking). Check in 5 minutes before arrival.

**Verdict key:** ✅ Carried — same product/brand stocked at Whole Foods · 🔄 Substitute —
brand not carried, named replacement · ❌ Not available — keep buying on Amazon ·
❓ Unverified — could not confirm, check in the app
